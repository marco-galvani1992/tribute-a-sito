The day

Music for
the whole day

From the ceremony to the last dance we take care of the music, so you can simply enjoy your wedding.

  1. Acoustic ceremony and aperitivo

    Voice and guitar for your entrance, the rings and the toast. You choose the songs, we fit them to your moments.

    Your song, played by us, at the moment you choose.

    A medley of about fifteen minutes for every decade, from the '60s to today's hits, one song flowing into the next.

  4. DJ set until late

    When the band stops, the party goes on: DJ set on request, with the lights and sound already in place.

FAQ

Before you write

Do you play at weddings for couples from abroad?

Yes. We have played for international couples and we are happy to speak English with you, your wedding planner and your guests, on WhatsApp and on stage.

What music do you play?

The hits that get everyone dancing, from the '60s to today: Italian and international pop, rock and dance, in medleys by decade. We gladly add your favourite songs.

How long is the show?

Two hours of live music in blocks of about fifteen minutes per decade. Before that, if you like, an acoustic set for the ceremony and the aperitivo; afterwards, a DJ set.

Can you play our first dance?

Yes, live, at the moment you choose.

Do you bring sound and lights?

Yes: sound and lights are included in our packages, with a sound engineer in the most complete ones.

What do you need at the venue?

A stage or space of at least 4x3 metres, 220V power with two or three sockets, cover if we play outdoors, a place to change, and parking or a loading area near the entrance. We arrive one to two hours early to set up and soundcheck.

Where do you play?

We are based in Verona and play all over Lake Garda (Sirmione, Bardolino, Lazise, Garda, Desenzano, Riva del Garda…), Verona and Valpolicella, Veneto, Lombardy and Trentino. For other places, just ask.

What about music licensing (SIAE)?

In Italy the SIAE music permit is paid by the organiser: we will tell you exactly what is needed and when.
